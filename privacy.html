Privacy

Last changed 29 September 2026. This covers the game on the web and the Android app.

Your household lives on your device. Nothing goes to a server unless you turn on cloud save.

No ads. No analytics. No third-party trackers. Nothing is sold, rented or swapped, including you.

What stays on your device

The household: residents, drawings, notes, keepsakes, progress and settings. The browser keeps it in its local storage (under shelflife.v4); the Android app keeps it in its own storage. It does not leave the device unless you send it somewhere: a backup file, a postcard or cloud save. Backups and postcards go only where you send them.

Clearing the site's data, or uninstalling the app, removes it. If Android's own backup is switched on, your phone may include the app's storage in the backup it keeps in your Google account. That is between you and Google; we never see it.

What reaches a server, if you turn on cloud save

Cloud save is off until you choose More → Cloud save → Turn on cloud save. Guest play never needs an account. With it on, these are stored:

  • The save. The same data as a backup file, so your shelf can follow you to another device.
  • Your email address, only if you add one. It is used to send the 6 digit sign-in code and for nothing else. There are no passwords.
  • A random device id, such as android-k2x9q4mz, so the game can tell your own devices apart. It is made up by the game, not read from your phone.
  • An account id, a friend code and the name you choose, plus when the save was last written.

Friends

If you use the friend features, the server also keeps:

  • Friend codes and friend lists. Nobody is added without accepting.
  • Shelf snapshots, shared only with friends you have accepted.
  • Shelf Court summonses sent between friends.
  • Daily arcade scores. The names beside them are visible only to your friends.

There are no public profiles, no chat and no strangers. Other players see nothing of yours unless you have accepted them as a friend.

Who holds it

Cloud save runs on Supabase, which stores the data and sends the sign-in emails on our behalf, and handles them only to provide that service. The sign-in session stays on your device under shelflife.cloud, never inside the save or its backups.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting services or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party trackers or SDKs of any kind. The fonts ship with the game.
  • No selling or sharing of data, for marketing or anything else.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Notifications and the narrator

Nudges in the Android app are local notifications. The phone schedules them from what the game already knows; there is no push server and nothing is sent anywhere. They stay off until you switch them on.

The narrator uses your device's own voices. Some browsers also offer online voices that send the text to the browser's maker to be spoken; the voice list marks those as online.

Children

Shelf Life is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is darkly comic: cartoon peril, curses and a great many funerals. We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13, and cloud save is not for them. If a child under 13 has made an account, ask us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting

Cloud data is kept while the account exists. You can delete it at any time, with or without the app: see delete your account. That removes the account, your email, the cloud save, the device id, your friend code and name, friend links, shared snapshots, summonses and scores. Copies in the provider's routine backups expire on its normal schedule. The household on your device is left alone.

To see the cloud save itself, choose More → Back up: the file holds the same data.

Questions

Ask through the issue form. Issues are public, so please do not post your email address there. Say you need a private word and we will arrange one.
