# Rug and household implementation evidence

The rug now uses bounded reaction (0.22s, 0.42s for watchful traits), acceleration (850 world units/s²), speed (310, or 370 for feral/bitey), a hand/mouth contact region and speed-based grip. A dropped catch follows a recoverable trajectory; a missed throw stays visible before settling away. Catches keep the ball for 1.15 seconds and return the same entity, immune to repeat catch rewards. Refusals follow rapid simultaneous throws for particular traits and are signaled. Free play remains untimed. Optional three-catch, bounce and high challenges use actual catch events.

Ball geometry and rendering share a radius computed from stage width, targeting 32 CSS pixels; the SVG padding was removed. No saved creature art was changed. Fresh arrival drafts use bone/mould and stitches. Existing player appearance remains player-owned.

Structured rug lines cover catches, fumbles, recovery, misses, refusal, bubbles, return and jump, with trait variants and actual event callbacks. Echoes retain 24 bounded records, throttle repeated same-kind events, and survive normalization/backup. Care, welcome, court, market and outings connect to household aftermath. First feed or welcome supplies an early crumb funeral and visitor; Coffin Bed is available immediately. Several previously soft story endings retain their premise through a bodily or macabre payoff.
