A small field guide

Nobody here came with instructions. These are our best guesses.

  1. Deal with the emergencies.

    Something goes wrong on the shelf every twelve minutes, and up to three disasters pile up while you are away. Each one is a choice. Every choice pays souls; not every choice goes well. Spend souls on coffins, collect cursed curios, and watch the neighbours’ opinion of your house get worse. Turn over one omen a night and finish three unholy chores a day for extra.

  2. Make someone peculiar.

    Grow a creature and change its parts, or draw one and give it moving stamps. Name it. Move it in.

  3. Keep it fed, fussed, and clean.

    Tap a creature to care for it. Helping when a need is below 72 earns care credit; every three credits adds a trust point. Rounds help everyone a little, but build no trust. Games and conspiracies add at most three trust per resident per day; care is the steady route.

  4. Read the room.

    Check the shelf for notes, arguments, and small developments. A resident files at most one grievance an hour, so reading often costs nothing. Individual care builds trust, unlocking drawing tools and furniture in Decorate. Tap a byline on the board to keep one resident’s notes.

  5. Reconsider the neighbours.

    Drag to rearrange. On a phone, hold first. Or open a resident’s card and choose a shelf position. Furniture affects the creatures beside it; some neighbours feud. Watch them: they nudge, whisper, glare and steal from whoever is next to them.

  6. Encourage a terrible idea.

    Small conspiracies offer choices with visible costs and rewards. Help, redirect, or leave the residents to their own devices. They act after three minutes; another plan follows five minutes later. Nobody dies. Sometimes the raisin does.

  7. Make a little history.

    Stories holds a six-beat weekly case and temporary visitors. Follow the evidence through care, placement and choices. A case waits until you finish it. Visitors stay six hours and leave museum curiosities when welcomed.

  8. Keep your promises.

    Accept a resident’s request in its card, then fulfil it within twelve hours of accepting for trust. Refusing costs one trust and adds a grievance. Friends form after fifteen minutes beside each other with trust on both sides. Rivals can reach a truce once both trust you at level three.

  9. Find your kind of mischief.

    Open Play on a phone or Playroom on a larger screen. Choose a resident, then a game: catch crumbs in Chase, remember a Handshake, prove a lie in Alibi, recover building parts on an Expedition, solve Shelf Court, or pair purchases for Night Market errands. Each game explains its goal and rewards. Practice whenever you like; taking a break costs nothing.

Needs change while you are away, up to eighteen hours at a time, and only half as fast at night. They cannot die. You can take a break. Your shelf saves on this device; More → Back up keeps a copy you can restore elsewhere.

Keyboard: Tab through controls, Enter or Space to activate, Escape to close a sheet. The game respects your device’s reduced-motion preference.

Choose Mail, another device, or Files if offered. The backup may be a .json or .txt file; Restore accepts both.

On the other device

  1. Save the email attachment or shared file.
  2. Open Shelf Life and choose More → Restore.
  3. Select the file and check the preview before replacing the shelf.

Back up any shelf already on the other device first. This is a snapshot, not automatic sync: send a fresh backup from the device you last played on whenever you switch.
