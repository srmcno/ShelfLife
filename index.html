A small field guide

Nobody here came with instructions. These are our best guesses.

  1. Make someone peculiar.

    Grow a creature and change its parts, or draw one and give it moving stamps. Name it. Move it in.

  2. Keep it fed, fussed, and clean.

    Tap a creature to care for it. Helping when a need is below 72 earns care credit; every three credits adds a trust point. Rounds help everyone a little, but build no trust.

  3. Read the room.

    Check the shelf for notes, arguments, and small developments. Individual care builds trust, unlocking drawing tools and furniture in Decorate. The progress strip shows your next discovery.

  4. Reconsider the neighbours.

    Drag to rearrange. On a phone, hold first. Or open a resident’s card and choose a shelf position. Furniture affects the creatures beside it; some neighbours feud.

  5. Encourage a terrible idea.

    Small conspiracies offer choices with visible costs and rewards. Help, redirect, or leave the residents to their own devices. They act after three minutes; another plan follows five minutes later. Nobody dies. Sometimes the raisin does.

Needs change while you are away, up to two days at a time. They cannot die. You can take a break. Your shelf saves on this device; More → Back up keeps a copy you can restore elsewhere.

Keyboard: Tab through controls, Enter or Space to activate, Escape to close a sheet. The game respects your device’s reduced-motion preference.
