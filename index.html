A small field guide

Nobody here came with instructions. These are our best guesses.

  1. Make someone peculiar.

    Grow a creature and change its parts, or draw one and give it moving stamps. Name it. Move it in.

  2. Keep it fed, fussed, and clean.

    Tap a creature to care for it. Helping when a need is below 72 earns care credit; every three credits adds a trust point. Rounds help everyone a little, but build no trust. Games and conspiracies add at most three trust per resident per day; care is the steady route.

  3. Read the room.

    Check the shelf for notes, arguments, and small developments. A resident files at most one grievance an hour, so reading often costs nothing. Individual care builds trust, unlocking drawing tools and furniture in Decorate. Tap a byline on the board to keep one resident’s notes.

  4. Reconsider the neighbours.

    Drag to rearrange. On a phone, hold first. Or open a resident’s card and choose a shelf position. Furniture affects the creatures beside it; some neighbours feud. Watch them: they nudge, whisper, glare and steal from whoever is next to them.

  5. Encourage a terrible idea.

    Small conspiracies offer choices with visible costs and rewards. Help, redirect, or leave the residents to their own devices. They act after three minutes; another plan follows five minutes later. Nobody dies. Sometimes the raisin does.

  6. Make a little history.

    Plots holds a six-beat weekly case and temporary visitors. Follow the evidence through care, placement and choices. A case waits until you finish it. Visitors stay six hours and leave museum curiosities when welcomed.

  7. Keep your promises.

    Accept a resident’s request in its card, then fulfil it within twelve hours of accepting for trust. Refusing costs one trust and adds a grievance. Friends form after fifteen minutes beside each other with trust on both sides. Rivals can reach a truce once both trust you at level three.

  8. Learn their secret handshake.

    A short untimed memory game lives in each resident’s card. Repeat three patterns for attention and trust. Wrong taps cost nothing; replay the pattern. Rewards recharge every five minutes per resident; practice whenever you like.

Needs change while you are away, up to eighteen hours at a time, and only half as fast at night. They cannot die. You can take a break. Your shelf saves on this device; More → Back up keeps a copy you can restore elsewhere.
