Delete your account

You can delete your Shelf Life cloud account, and everything it keeps on the server, with or without the app.

What goes

The account, your email address, the cloud copy of your shelf, the device ids, your friend code and name, your friend links, shared shelf snapshots, Shelf Court summonses and arcade scores. All of it, at once, for good. There is no undo.

What stays

The household on your phone or in your browser. It lives on the device, not the server, and stays until you clear it: clear the site's data in the browser, or uninstall the app. Back it up first if you might want it.

In the game

  1. Open the game at srmcno.github.io/ShelfLife in any browser, or open the app.
  2. Choose More, then Cloud save.
  3. If this device is not signed in, choose Already have an account? Sign in on this device and enter the code we email you. On a browser with an empty shelf this brings your household there too, and it stays after the account has gone.
  4. Choose Delete my cloud data and account, then Delete for good.

Or here

Sign in with the email on your account and delete it from this page. This does not touch the game or its sign-in in this browser.

Deleted. The account and everything the server held for it are gone. Any shelf on your devices is still there, and entirely yours.

This edition of the game has no cloud save switched on, so there is no account on a server to delete. Your household lives only on your device.

If you cannot sign in

An account without an email can only be opened from the browser or phone that made it. If that has gone, or the code never arrives, ask through the issue form and say it is a deletion request. Issues are public, so please do not post your email address there: we will arrange a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it.
